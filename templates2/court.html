📄 Legal Help

📋 FIR ke baad basic process

FIR ke baad investigation, evidence collection aur legal/court proceedings ho sakti hain. Exact process case ke facts par depend karta hai.

⚖️ Bail kya hoti hai?

Bail ek legal process hai jisme applicable circumstances aur law ke according accused ko custody se release kiya ja sakta hai.

📝 Complaint aur FIR me difference

Complaint kisi authority ko di gayi information/ grievance ho sakti hai. FIR police ke dwara cognizable offence ki information record karne ki specific process hai.

🏛️ Court me case ka basic process

Case ke type ke according filing, hearings, evidence, arguments aur order/judgment jaise stages ho sakte hain.

📁 Important Documents

Case ke according FIR copy, identification documents, notices, relevant evidence aur court documents ki zarurat ho sakti hai.
