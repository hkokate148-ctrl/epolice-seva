About ePolice Seva

📢 Digital Police Assistance & Public Service Platform

📌 About the Project

ePolice Seva is a digital platform designed to provide easy access to police-related services and useful public information through a single website.

The platform aims to make it easier for users to access services such as online complaints, emergency information, complaint status, court and legal information, and contact support.

🎯 Our Purpose

  • Provide easy access to police-related services.
  • Provide emergency helpline information.
  • Allow users to submit complaints online.
  • Provide complaint/status-related information.
  • Provide basic court and legal information.
  • Make important public-service information easier to find.

🚨 Emergency Services

The Emergency section provides important emergency helpline numbers. In an immediate emergency, users should contact the appropriate official emergency service.

⚖️ Legal Information

Legal information provided on this website is for general informational purposes. It should not be treated as a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.

📞 Contact Us

Users can contact ePolice Seva through the Contact section to report website-related problems, suggestions, or questions.

Note: ePolice Seva does not replace official police, court, emergency, or government services. For urgent matters, contact the appropriate official authority directly.
